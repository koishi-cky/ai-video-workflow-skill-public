# Design truth

Concept: a calm, credible AI workbench that behaves like real desktop software rather than a text-card animation.

- Canvas: 1920x1080, 30fps, 10 seconds, silent.
- Palette: background `#07100c`, shell `#111814`, panel `#161f1a`, foreground `#f2efe5`, muted `#a9b2aa`, accent `#c8ad62`.
- Typography: locally available CJK sans-serif fonts through the `PublicCN` fallback family; hierarchy comes from scale, weight and spacing.
- Focal element: the bottom goal composer, followed by the generated three-step plan.
- Edge anchors: navigation at left, window title at top, processing status at upper right.
- Background: restrained warm-gold radial glow and vignette; no grid, particles or ornamental English.
- Motion: the outer Mac-style window remains fixed. Only its inner workspace zooms toward the input and returns after send.
- Avoid: narration copied as a headline, fake brand UI, fake completion claims, persistent camera drift, overlapping old/new states.
